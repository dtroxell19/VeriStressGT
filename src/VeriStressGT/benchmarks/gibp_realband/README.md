# gibp_realband — constructors tuned to real-network IBP difficulty

A mini benchmark suite of the constructor families whose **IBP relative gap (G_IBP)** responds to their
amplitude hyperparameter, tuned toward the **real verified-robust band** (median 59, P25–P75 = **[9.3,
467]**; full-real up to ~3.6M). It contains only the four *amplifier-type* families — the four
intentionally IBP-tight families (deep-contractive, embedded-projection, and both attention
constructors, all G_IBP ≈ 0) are excluded because they cannot and are not meant to reach real G_IBP.

12 instances (3 per family), generated by `configs/gibp_push.yaml` and profiled with
`difficulty_profile`:

**Reading:** all four families reach the real band via a monotone amplitude knob. **Paired-bias** lands
cleanly inside it at every setting (103–369). **Corners** (`hinge_l1`), **MILP** (near-boundary ε), and
**MEAP** (`num_pairs`) sweep through the band and overshoot at the high end — so to sit at the real
*median* they should be dialed toward the lower settings (e.g. corners `hinge_l1≈1000`, MEAP
`num_pairs≈8–16`). The overshooting instances are kept here to document the tunable range.

Ready-to-use "real-median G_IBP" settings: paired-bias `num_pairs=32, num_backbone_layers=4,
margin=0.001`; corners `hinge_l1=1000`; MILP near-boundary at `h=20`.

Reproduce: `PYTHONPATH=src python -m VeriStressGT.cli.create_benchmark --spec
src/VeriStressGT/configs/gibp_push.yaml --out_dir rebuttal_materials/gibp_push --overwrite`, then filter
to the meap/milp/rc/pb families (this suite).
